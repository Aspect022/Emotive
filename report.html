4 Critical Evaluation of Prior Proposals

During Step 1 research, a "Tri-S²Former" tri-branch architecture was proposed. Before proceeding, we conducted an honest, researcher-level evaluation of each component. The key principle: every component must have a clear, defensible justification tied to a specific problem in our constraint set.

BranchClaimReality CheckVerdict
Dynamic Graph Attention Captures functional connectivity between 14 electrode nodes With only 14 nodes, the adjacency matrix is 14×14 = 196 learnable parameters. Graph Neural Networks are designed for hundreds to thousands of nodes. At 14 nodes, a standard attention mechanism achieves the same spatial modeling without graph abstraction overhead. Risk of overfitting edge weights. ⚠️ Unjustified complexity
Riemannian SPD Manifold Covariance matrices lie on SPD manifold; geodesic metrics strip inter-subject noise Mathematically correct and well-supported by 2024–2026 literature (Tibermacine et al., Jin et al., Gao & Deng). 14×14 covariance matrices are the right dimensionality — stable to estimate, small enough for efficient manifold operations. ✅ Genuinely justified
Temporal Conformer + Wavelet Multi-scale temporal modeling of neuro-oscillations Every EEG deep learning paper does temporal modeling. No specific argument for why multi-scale helps for these 5 cognitive tasks vs. standard temporal convolution. ❌ Generic, not novel
Lesson learned: The Tri-S²Former was engineer-optimized (stack impressive modules), not researcher-optimized (justify every component). Three branches triple the parameter count and overfitting risk on 37K samples without proven independent contribution. A simpler, well-justified architecture is stronger than a complex, unjustified one.

5 The Real Novel Contribution

Instead of claiming novelty through architectural complexity, we claim novelty through a coherent, end-to-end system that no prior work has demonstrated. The contribution is organized into three layers, each solving a distinct problem:

🧬 Layer 1: Riemannian Geometry-Aware EEG Encoding (Methodological Novelty)

What: Replace handcrafted PSD/DE features with learned spatial covariance representations on the Symmetric Positive Definite (SPD) manifold.

Why justified: The 14×14 covariance matrix is naturally SPD. Riemannian metrics are mathematically invariant to the linear transformations caused by skull thickness, electrode impedance, and cortical geometry differences — our primary noise source.

What it improves: Eliminates the manual feature engineering bottleneck and provides fundamentally more robust representations than Euclidean neural networks.

📊 Layer 2: Evidential Uncertainty-Calibrated Classification (Architectural Novelty)

What: Replace standard softmax with a Dirichlet distribution head that outputs calibrated probabilities AND explicit uncertainty mass.

Why justified: When the output feeds into career recommendations, the difference between "confident: 80% working memory" and "uncertain: maybe 50–80% working memory" is the difference between a useful system and a dangerous one. Standard softmax gives wildly overconfident scores on small datasets. No EEG cognitive classification paper has used EDL for aptitude profiling.

What it enables: The system can say "I don't know" (high vacuity $u$) instead of giving a spurious confident answer.

🎯 Layer 3: Psychometrically-Grounded Career Mapping (System Novelty)

What: Map the Dirichlet cognitive profile through CHC (Cattell-Horn-Carroll) cognitive ability theory into O*NET occupational requirements, producing career fit scores with Bayesian credible intervals.

Why justified: CHC theory is the most validated cognitive ability taxonomy in psychometrics (70+ years of research). O*NET is the US Department of Labor's standardized occupational database with ability requirements for 900+ occupations.

What makes it novel: No prior system connects real-time EEG classification → CHC abilities → O*NET career mapping with uncertainty propagation.

6.2 Riemannian SPD Encoder — Mathematical Detail

The core encoder operates on the Symmetric Positive Definite (SPD) manifold $\mathcal{S}_{++}^{14}$ rather than Euclidean space. This is not a design choice — it is a mathematical necessity. Spatial covariance matrices of EEG channels are inherently SPD, and applying Euclidean operations (standard CNNs, flat MLPs) distorts their intrinsic geometry.

Step 1 — Spatial Covariance Matrix:

$$\Sigma = \frac{1}{T-1}(X - \bar{X})(X - \bar{X})^\top + \epsilon I \in \mathcal{S}_{++}^{14}$$

Step 2 — BiMap Layer (Riemannian fully connected): Projects the SPD matrix to a lower-dimensional manifold via semi-orthogonal weight matrices on the Stiefel manifold:

$$\Sigma^{(l+1)} = W_l \Sigma^{(l)} W_l^\top, \quad W_l \in \mathrm{St}(d_l, d_{l-1})$$

Dimensionality reduction path: 14 → 8 → 6, preserving positive-definiteness at every layer.

Step 3 — ReEig Layer (Riemannian ReLU): Prevents eigenvalue collapse near the manifold boundary:

$$\Sigma' = U \max(\Lambda, \epsilon I) U^\top$$

Step 4 — LogEig Layer (Manifold → Euclidean): Projects the manifold representation into flat tangent space for downstream classification:

$$v = \mathrm{vec}\left(\log(\Sigma')\right) = \mathrm{vec}\left(U \log(\Lambda) U^\top\right)$$

Why this matters for inter-subject robustness: Variations in skull thickness, electrode impedance, and global amplitude scaling between participants manifest as linear transformations of the covariance matrix. Riemannian geodesic distances are invariant to such transformations — meaning the SPD encoder inherently strips away a large portion of the subject-specific "neural fingerprint" without any adversarial training or explicit domain adaptation.

6.3 Evidential Deep Learning Head — Mathematical Detail

Instead of standard softmax (which squashes logits into overconfident point estimates), the final layer outputs non-negative evidence values that parameterize a Dirichlet distribution over class probabilities:

Evidence to Dirichlet parameters:

$$\alpha_k = e_k + 1, \quad S = \sum_{k=1}^{K} \alpha_k$$

Expected class probability:

$$\hat{p}_k = \frac{\alpha_k}{S}$$

Epistemic uncertainty (vacuity):

$$u = \frac{K}{S} \in (0, 1]$$

ScenarioEvidenceDirichlet Strength SUncertainty uInterpretation
Total ignorance (new/unseen pattern)$e_k = 0 \;\forall k$$S = K = 5$$u = 1.0$"I have no idea" → refuse to recommend
High confidence (familiar pattern)$e_{\text{math}} = 95$$S \gg K$$u \approx 0.05$"High confidence: arithmetic task" → recommend
Conflicting evidence (noisy signal)$e_k \approx 10 \;\forall k$$S = 55$$u = 0.09$Low uncertainty but flat → signal quality flag

Evidential Loss Function (Sensoy et al., 2018):

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{EDL}} = \sum_{k=1}^{K} y_k \left(\psi(S) - \psi(\alpha_k)\right) + \lambda_t \cdot \text{KL}\left[\text{Dir}(\tilde{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}) \,\|\, \text{Dir}(\mathbf{1})\right]$$

where $\psi(\cdot)$ is the Digamma function, $\tilde{\alpha}_k = y_k + (1-y_k)\alpha_k$, and $\lambda_t = \min(1, t/10)$ anneals the KL regularizer over the first 10 epochs.

6.4 Complete Loss Function

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{EDL}}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}, \mathbf{y}) + \lambda_1 \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}^{\text{EEG-only}} + \lambda_2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{center}}$$

TermPurpose
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{EDL}}$Evidential classification with KL regularizer. Penalizes overconfidence and drives uncertain samples toward a uniform prior.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}^{\text{EEG-only}}$Auxiliary EEG-only branch prevents behavioral metadata shortcut learning. Guarantees the model works without behavioral data.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{center}}$Pulls tangent-space representations toward class centroids: $\sum_i \|v_i - c_{y_i}\|_2^2$. Improves manifold clustering.

7 Cognitive-to-Career Mapping Layer

7.1 Step 1 — EEG Tasks → CHC Cognitive Abilities

The mapping is grounded in Cattell-Horn-Carroll (CHC) theory, the most validated cognitive ability taxonomy in psychometrics (McGrew 2009, Keith & Reynolds 2010). CHC organizes human cognitive abilities into a three-stratum hierarchy: General Intelligence ($g$) → 10 Broad Abilities → 70+ Narrow Abilities.

Working MemoryShort-Term Working Memory$G_{wm}$Memorization, Information OrderingATC, Emergency Response, Logistics

*Important caveat: Sustained Attention is not a clean CHC broad ability factor. It maps most closely to Processing Speed ($G_s$) via the WJ-III Pair Cancellation task, but it is more accurately described as an executive function / arousal construct. This distinction must be explicitly acknowledged in the paper.

7.2 Step 2 — CHC Abilities → O*NET Career Fit Scores

O*NET (US Dept. of Labor) provides importance and level ratings for 52 cognitive abilities across 900+ occupations. Our 5 EEG-derived abilities map to 5 of these 52 descriptors.

Career fit score (point estimate):

Let $\mathbf{p} \sim \text{Dir}(\boldsymbol{\alpha})$ be the EEG-derived cognitive profile. Let $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{5 \times J}$ be the normalized O*NET requirement matrix.

$$\text{Fit}_j = \mathbf{p}^\top \mathbf{w}_j = \sum_{k=1}^5 p_k \cdot W_{kj}$$

With uncertainty propagation (Monte Carlo):

  1. Draw $N = 1000$ samples: $\mathbf{p}^{(n)} \sim \text{Dir}(\boldsymbol{\alpha})$
  2. Compute $\text{Fit}_j^{(n)} = \mathbf{p}^{(n)\top} \mathbf{w}_j$ for each sample
  3. Report: $\text{Fit}_j = \mathbb{E}[\text{Fit}_j^{(n)}]$ and 95% CI = $[Q_{2.5\%}, Q_{97.5\%}]$

Abstention rule:

When epistemic uncertainty $u > u_{\text{threshold}}$ (e.g., $u > 0.5$), the system outputs: "Insufficient confidence in cognitive profile. Consider additional testing sessions."

8 Validation Strategy

8.1 The Three-Tier Validation Ladder

Following the biomarker translation framework (Clayson 2024, 2025; Parmigiani 2022), validation must progress through three tiers:

1
Tier 1: Analytic & Signal Validity
  • Split-half reliability of EEG features
  • Test-retest ICC across sessions (same subject)
  • Signal quality metrics (SNR, artifact rates)
2
Tier 2: Classification & Calibration Validity
  • 5-class accuracy, Macro-F1, per-class AUROC
  • Expected Calibration Error (ECE ↓), Brier Score
  • Reliability diagrams, uncertainty quality (OOD detection AUROC)
  • Permutation test (p < 0.001), bootstrap confidence intervals
3
Tier 3: Construct & Decision-Support Validity
  • Convergent validity: EEG profile vs. standardized cognitive tests (WJ-IV, WAIS subtests)
  • Career mapping face validity: expert panel review
  • Risk-coverage curves for abstention utility
  • Longitudinal: profile stability over weeks

9 Ethical & Legal Constraints

EEG-based cognitive profiling touches fundamental questions of neuroethics, privacy, and algorithmic fairness. All four Step 2 research reports converge on the same conclusion: the system must be positioned as advisory decision-support, never as a hiring or screening tool.

Regulatory FrameworkKey ConstraintOur Response
EU AI Act (2024)AI in recruitment/vocational selection = High-Risk (Annex III). Article 5 prohibits workplace emotion recognition and biometric categorization.Position as voluntary self-discovery and career exploration guidance — never as hiring/screening tool.
US ADA / EEOC (2024)EEG is classified as a medical examination. Pre-offer medical exams are prohibited. Cognitive profiles could inadvertently reveal neurodivergence (ADHD, learning disabilities).Never frame as employment screening. System is for educational and personal career exploration only.
GDPR (EU)EEG data is biometric/health data (special category under Article 9). Requires explicit informed consent and data minimization.Require explicit opt-in consent. Minimize data retention. Process on-device where possible.

9.1 Mandatory Disclaimers in the Paper

  1. "This system is designed as an exploratory decision-support tool for career self-discovery, not an employment screening instrument."
  2. "Cognitive ability profiles are influenced by many factors beyond neural measurements, including motivation, prior experience, cultural context, and test-taking conditions."
  3. "Career success depends on non-cognitive factors (personality, interests, motivation, socioeconomic context) not measured by this system."

9.2 The "Neuro-Essentialism" Fallacy

There is a flawed assumption that biological neural signals are inherently superior or more "truthful" than behavioral task performance. If an individual solves complex math problems via an idiosyncratic cognitive strategy that does not produce standard parietal-frontal arithmetic EEG signatures, penalizing their STEM aptitude score would represent a severe failure of construct validity. The paper must acknowledge this limitation explicitly.
